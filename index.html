    How each piece is built
    Web chat: a widget on the Shopify storefront. It knows which product page the shopper is on and what is in the cart. Product cards: built from live Shopify product data. On the site they can hold any layout; in RCS a card is one image, a title, a description and up to 4 buttons. Buttons: add to cart and checkout on the site; suggested replies and open-link actions in RCS. RCS: sent from a verified Galway sender, only to shoppers who opted in. No RCS? The same conversation goes out as SMS, with links in place of cards.
    Sales agent powered by 21X, formerly Structurely

    How it works

    More than a chat window. An agent that can act.

    Answering a sizing question is the easy part. The sale closes when the right size lands in the cart, and the exchange happens when the label is already in the customer's hand. The agent pairs conversation with Galway's own content and store systems, and your team keeps control of anything unusual.

    Store knowledge

    Galway's own answers

    The catalog, the size and fit guide, shipping, the 30-day return window, one free exchange per order and Galway Rewards. It quotes what Galway has published and never invents a price or a discount.

    Conversational AI

    Sells like your best rep

    Understands "42 chest, but I want a sweater under it", asks the one question that settles the size, and takes no for an answer when a shopper passes on an add-on.

    Store integration

    Reads and writes back

    Shopify for products, stock, carts and orders. The returns portal for exchanges and labels. Rewards for points. It works where each system allows it.

    Team inbox

    Handoffs with context

    Custom orders, damaged items and anything outside policy go to a person, with the conversation and the order attached. The shopper is told who has it.

    Galway's site already has an Ask AI assistant. This pilot is about what happens after the answer: the cart, the follow-up text and the exchange. Which systems the agent can write to is confirmed in discovery, not assumed here.

    Controlled pilot

    Start with the four moments that decide the sale.

    1. Size and fit on the product pageThe shopper who toggles between L and XL gets an answer from the size guide before they leave.
    2. Cart recovery, in chat and by textAn open cart gets one opted-in reminder with the items, the price and a checkout link.
    3. Building the kitThe right jacket for the conditions, then the pant and layer that go with it, offered once.
    4. Exchange before refundAsk why, offer the size that fixes it, send the label. The refund is always still available.
    5. Order status and policy questionsWhere is my order, shipping times, care instructions. Answered at any hour.
